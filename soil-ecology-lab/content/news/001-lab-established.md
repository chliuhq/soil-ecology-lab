## 中文

2025年7月，刘华清博士正式加入广西大学林学院，杨佳慧博士正式加入广西大学农学院，共同成立土壤生态与水土保持课题组。课题组聚焦植物多样性与土壤水碳耦合、土壤有机碳形成与转化、土壤侵蚀与水土保持、多源遥感定量监测等前沿方向，欢迎有志青年加入！

## English

In July 2025, Dr. Huaqing Liu joined the College of Forestry and Dr. Jiahui Yang joined the College of Agriculture at Guangxi University, co-founding the Soil Ecology and Conservation Lab. The lab focuses on plant diversity–soil water-carbon coupling, soil organic carbon dynamics, soil erosion and conservation, and multi-source remote sensing monitoring. We welcome aspiring students to join!
