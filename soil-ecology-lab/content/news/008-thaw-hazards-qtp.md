## 中文

杨佳慧等人在《Global and Planetary Change》发表题为“Spatial variability and future susceptibility of thaw hazards on hillslopes across the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau”的研究论文，文章编号为105647，发表于2026年8月13日的Version of Record版本。论文DOI为：https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gloplacha.2026.105647。

青藏高原多年冻土区广泛分布着山坡解冻危害，对区域景观稳定性和基础设施安全构成潜在威胁。为系统评估这类危害的空间分布及未来变化，研究团队基于文献资料建立了山坡解冻危害数据集，并通过目视解译识别非解冻危害样本。在此基础上，研究选取18个环境预测因子，利用9种机器学习和深度学习模型，对青藏高原多年冻土区山坡解冻危害的空间易感性进行了评估。

研究结果表明，NDVI、降水季节性、年均降水量和年均地温是影响山坡解冻危害易感性的关键环境预测因子。随机森林、Bagging、XGBoost和Gradient Boosting模型均取得了较高的预测性能，AUC均超过0.95。研究团队进一步采用SHAP方法解释模型预测结果，识别不同环境因子对山坡解冻危害易感性的相对贡献，并以这4种高性能模型预测结果的统计集成均值作为最终易感性预测结果，以降低单一模型带来的预测偏差。

空间分布结果显示，高易感性和极高易感性区域约占多年冻土区面积的5.53%，主要分布于青藏高原中部和东北部；低易感性和极低易感性区域占多年冻土区面积的89.39%，主要分布于青藏高原南部。未来情景预测表明，低易感性和极低易感性区域将略有扩大，而高易感性和极高易感性区域有所减少。

本研究建立了较为完善的山坡解冻危害综合评价体系，研究结果可为青藏高原山坡解冻危害清单编制、寒区地质灾害制图以及北半球寒区风险评估提供科学依据。

## English

Jiahui Yang and colleagues published a research article entitled “Spatial variability and future susceptibility of thaw hazards on hillslopes across the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau” in *Global and Planetary Change*. The article, numbered 105647, was published as the Version of Record on August 13, 2026. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gloplacha.2026.105647.

Hillslope thaw hazards are widely distributed in the permafrost region of the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau and may threaten regional landscape stability and infrastructure safety. To systematically assess their spatial distribution and future changes, the research team developed a hillslope thaw-hazard dataset from published literature and identified non-thaw-hazard samples through visual interpretation. Eighteen environmental predictors were then selected, and nine machine-learning and deep-learning models were used to assess thaw-hazard susceptibility across the permafrost region of the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau.

The results showed that NDVI, precipitation seasonality, mean annual precipitation, and mean annual ground temperature were key environmental predictors of hillslope thaw-hazard susceptibility. Random Forest, Bagging, XGBoost, and Gradient Boosting achieved high predictive performance, with AUC values exceeding 0.95. The team further applied SHAP analysis to interpret the model predictions and identify the relative contributions of different environmental factors. The statistical ensemble mean of the predictions from these four high-performing models was used as the final susceptibility estimate to reduce potential bias from any single model.

The spatial assessment indicated that areas with high and extremely high susceptibility accounted for approximately 5.53% of the permafrost region and were mainly distributed in the central and northeastern Qinghai-Tibet Plateau. Areas with low and extremely low susceptibility accounted for 89.39% of the permafrost region and were mainly distributed in the southern part of the plateau. Future projections suggested a slight expansion of low- and extremely low-susceptibility areas, accompanied by a reduction in high- and extremely high-susceptibility areas.

This study establishes an improved comprehensive framework for assessing hillslope thaw hazards. The findings provide a scientific basis for compiling thaw-hazard inventories on the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau, mapping geological hazards in cold regions, and assessing risks across the cold regions of the Northern Hemisphere.
