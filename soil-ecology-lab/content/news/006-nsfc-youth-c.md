## 中文

祝贺刘华清老师获批国家自然科学基金青年基金项目（C类），获批经费30万元，项目执行期为2027年1月至2029年12月，由刘华清老师主持。项目题目为「树种多样性对土壤有机碳垂向迁移与滞留的物理-生化耦合调节机制」。

## English

Congratulations to Dr. Huaqing Liu on receiving a National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) Youth Fund award (Category C), with funding of RMB 300,000. The project runs from January 2027 to December 2029 and is led by Dr. Huaqing Liu. The project is titled “Physical–biochemical coupled regulation mechanisms of soil organic carbon vertical transport and retention driven by tree species diversity.”
