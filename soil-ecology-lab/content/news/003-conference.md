## 中文

2025年8月16-20日，刘华清老师和杨佳慧老师赴西藏林芝参加中国土壤学会土壤侵蚀与水土保持专业委员会2025年学术年会，分别就植物多样性与土壤水碳耦合、多源遥感定量监测与水土保持等研究方向进行了学术交流。

## English

From August 16-20, 2025, Dr. Huaqing Liu and Dr. Jiahui Yang attended the 2025 Annual Meeting of the Soil Erosion and Water Conservation Committee, Soil Science Society of China, in Linzhi, Tibet. They presented on plant diversity–soil water-carbon coupling and multi-source remote sensing monitoring for soil-water conservation, respectively.
