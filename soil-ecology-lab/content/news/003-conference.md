## 中文

2025年10月，刘华清老师和杨佳慧老师赴会参加中国土壤学会2025年学术年会，分别就植物多样性与土壤水碳耦合、多源遥感定量监测与水土保持等研究方向进行了学术交流。

## English

In October 2025, Dr. Huaqing Liu and Dr. Jiahui Yang attended the 2025 Annual Meeting of the Soil Science Society of China, presenting on plant diversity–soil water-carbon coupling and multi-source remote sensing monitoring for soil-water conservation, respectively.
