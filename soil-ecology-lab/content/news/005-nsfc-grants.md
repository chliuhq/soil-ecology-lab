## 中文

祝贺刘华清老师和杨佳慧老师分别获批广西自然科学基金青年科学基金项目！刘华清老师主持的项目为「树种多样性调控多源DOM输入驱动土壤碳形成与稳定」，杨佳慧老师主持的项目为「广西坡耕地土壤侵蚀定量评价与未来趋势预测」，项目执行期均为2026年4月至2029年3月。

## English

Congratulations to Dr. Huaqing Liu and Dr. Jiahui Yang for each being awarded a Guangxi Natural Science Foundation Youth Project! Dr. Liu's project focuses on Tree Species Diversity Regulates Multi-source DOM Input Driving Soil Carbon Formation and Stabilization, while Dr. Yang's project addresses Quantitative Assessment and Future Trend Prediction of Soil Erosion on Sloping Farmland in Guangxi. Both projects run from April 2026 to March 2029.
